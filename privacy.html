How AltAmino handles information and how to contact us about your data.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to AltAmino (also written Altamino), its related websites and services, which are operated by the AltAmino Team.

To ask privacy-related questions, request help with personal information, or contact the team, use legal@support.altamino.top or our support portal.

Information we handle

Information you provide

  • Account information: your username, authentication information, and the details you add to your profile.
  • Content and communications: posts, chat messages, uploaded photos, videos, media and other user-created content.
  • Support and safety reports: the information included when you contact us, request assistance, or report a violation.

Technical information and permissions

  • Network and device information, which may include IP address, device information, app version and connection or error diagnostics used to operate and protect the service.
  • When a feature or integrated software component uses device permissions, information associated with those permissions may be accessed, including contacts, approximate location or precise location, depending on the app version, feature and device settings. Accessing data locally and sending it off the device are different operations; actual collection must reflect the specific app and SDK behavior.

Why information is used

Depending on the information and feature involved, we process information to:

  • Create accounts, authenticate sessions and provide profiles and communities.
  • Send, receive, display and store messages and user-generated content.
  • Keep the service functioning, diagnose technical problems and respond to abuse or security incidents.
  • Respond to support messages, enforce applicable rules and protect users.

We do not use the existence of an Android permission alone as proof that specific information is transmitted or retained. The data practices of third-party SDKs are also relevant to our disclosures.

When information is shared

Content you post publicly can be seen by other users. Messages or files you send can be viewed by their intended recipients, and recipients may retain content they receive.

To operate the platform, technical hosting, storage, connectivity or related providers may process information on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, lawful process or to respond to serious safety incidents. We do not represent these disclosures as an exhaustive list of any SDKs used by every app version.

Retention and account deletion

Account information and user content may be retained while needed to provide the service. Some records may be retained for legitimate safety, operational or legal reasons, including applicable backup and legal retention requirements.

To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact legal@support.altamino.top with the subject Account Deletion or use the support portal. Include your account username or another identifier, but never send your password. We may need to verify that you control the account.

We process deletion requests under applicable law, including removing associated user data when required, subject to lawful exceptions.

Your choices and requests

You can decide what profile details or content you share and whom you communicate with. Device settings allow you to manage permissions such as location and contact access. Where the app provides them, you can use account and content controls to update or delete information.

You may contact us to request assistance with accessing, correcting or deleting personal information, subject to applicable law.

Children and user safety

AltAmino prohibits the sexual abuse and exploitation of children. Read our Child Safety Standards for rules, reporting information and the designated child safety contact. Use the service only in accordance with applicable law and any age limits communicated through the app.

Security, updates and contact

We use safeguards appropriate to providing an online communication service and may update them as risks evolve. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

This policy may be updated when features, vendors, legal requirements or data handling practices change. The last-updated date at the top reflects the published revision.
