Our published standards

The AltAmino Team, responsible for AltAmino (Altamino), expressly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

It is forbidden to create, upload, solicit, store, distribute, promote or facilitate material or behavior involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children through our services.

Prohibited content and behavior

  • Creating, sharing, storing, requesting, linking to or distributing CSAM.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, coercion, sextortion or exploitation of minors.
  • Sexualized or exploitative imagery involving minors, including manipulated material.
  • Trafficking, threats, blackmail or coordination of abuse.
  • Using accounts, communities or chats to enable or encourage child exploitation.
  • Attempting to evade child safety enforcement.

Reporting child safety concerns

If you find suspected CSAE or CSAM, use the in-app report or feedback mechanism to report the account, content or behavior. You may also reach the safety team through the support portal or by emailing legal@support.altamino.top.

Include the account identifier, link and a brief description where possible. Do not download, copy, forward or redistribute suspected CSAM. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

Review and enforcement

Reports of suspected child sexual exploitation are serious safety matters. Where abuse is identified, action may include removing prohibited content, restricting or banning accounts, and taking other necessary steps to protect users and address the violation.

Confirmed CSAM must be addressed promptly after actual knowledge, in accordance with applicable law and our published standards.

Compliance with child safety laws

AltAmino commits to complying with applicable child safety laws. Where required, confirmed CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or an appropriate regional or national authority.

We respond to valid legal requests from competent authorities in accordance with applicable law.
