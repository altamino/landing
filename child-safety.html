1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Altamino explicitly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). We do not allow our services to be used to create, upload, distribute, request, promote, or facilitate content or conduct that sexually abuses or exploits children. These standards apply to all users, communications, communities, profiles, and content on Altamino.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly forbidden:

  • Creating, sharing, requesting, storing, linking to, or distributing CSAM.
  • Sexual grooming, solicitation, coercion, or exploitation of minors.
  • Sexualized depictions of minors, including exploitative or manipulated imagery.
  • Sextortion, threats, trafficking, or blackmail involving children.
  • Using accounts, groups, chats, or other features to encourage, coordinate, or facilitate the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Attempts to evade enforcement of these child safety standards.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter suspected CSAE or CSAM on Altamino, report it to the AltAmino Team using the contact information below. Include relevant account or content links and a brief description where possible. Do not download, forward, or redistribute suspected CSAM.

  • Child safety contact: legal@support.altamino.top
  • Support portal: support.altamino.top

Users should also use any reporting or support mechanism available within the Altamino app. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

4. Review and Enforcement

Reports of suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as serious safety matters. Content found to violate these standards is subject to removal, and accounts involved may be restricted, suspended, or permanently banned. Confirmed CSAM must be addressed promptly in accordance with applicable law, including its removal after actual knowledge of its presence.

5. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Altamino prohibits unlawful child exploitation and commits to complying with applicable child safety laws. Confirmed CSAM is to be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate regional or national authority, as required by applicable law. We cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities as required.
