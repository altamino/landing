About AltAmino

AltAmino (Altamino) is an alternative social and community platform provided by the AltAmino Team. Its services may offer user accounts, profiles, communities, chats, media sharing and related functionality.

These Terms cover the app and the related websites and services operated by the AltAmino Team.

Accounts and access

  • Use the service only when permitted by applicable law and any age requirements communicated in the app.
  • Keep access credentials confidential and do not impersonate other people.
  • Provide reasonably accurate account information where required for a feature.
  • Notify support if you think your account has been compromised.

Community conduct

AltAmino is a place for conversation and creativity, not abuse. Do not use the service for:

  • Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination or targeted hate.
  • Scams, fraud, impersonation, phishing, spam or malware.
  • Publishing another person's private information without authorization or lawful justification.
  • Violence, illegal activity or unlawful infringement of others' intellectual property.
  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) or Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), including grooming or sexual coercion of minors.

The Child Safety Standards provide more detail about these strictly prohibited activities.

Your content

You are responsible for the text, images, videos and other content you create or distribute through the service and must have permission to share it.

Posting publicly may make information available to other people. Messages delivered to recipients may remain accessible to them even if you later remove your own copy. Do not assume that deleting something from your device removes all copies elsewhere.

Reports and moderation

Accounts or content that violate these Terms may be reviewed and subject to proportionate action, including warnings, content removal, restricted access or account suspension. Serious child safety concerns are treated under our Child Safety Standards.

Use reporting tools available in the app or contact support.altamino.top if you need to report abuse or other concerns.

Privacy and deleting accounts

See our Privacy Policy for information about the types of data that may be processed and how to request changes or deletion.

You can request account deletion by contacting legal@support.altamino.top or support. Never include your password in an account deletion request.
